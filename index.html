Getting started

Tap Start Shift when you begin working and enter your odometer reading. Add earnings and expenses as they happen (or all at once at the end). Tap End Shift when you're done and enter the ending odometer - your summary shows exactly what you took home, per hour and per mile.

Forgot to track a shift?

Use Add Past Shift on the Home screen to record any earlier shift, then add its earnings and expenses. Every date and time in the app can be edited later.

Why does a statistic say "Not Available"?

Per-mile statistics need both odometer readings, and hourly statistics need a valid start and end time. When those are missing or inconsistent, My Ride Money says Not Available instead of guessing.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Your records live only on your device. Before switching phones or deleting the app, use Settings, then Export Backup and keep the file somewhere safe. On the new phone, Settings, then Import Backup restores everything - profiles, shifts, entries and settings.

Exports

Reports, then Create a PDF Report builds a shareable, printable PDF entirely on your phone. Settings offers CSV export of your shifts and entries for spreadsheets.
