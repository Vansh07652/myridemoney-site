My Ride Money

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

The short version: My Ride Money keeps your records on your device. There is no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no developer server holding your data. The app works completely offline.

What the app stores, and where

Your profiles, shifts, income entries, expense entries, odometer readings, notes and app settings are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. The developer does not operate a backend for user records and cannot see, receive, or recover your data.

What the app does not do

My Ride Money does not require an account or registration. It does not show ads or include any advertising SDK. It does not use behavioral analytics or tracking of any kind. It does not collect your location - the app never requests GPS access; miles are calculated only from odometer readings you type in yourself. It does not connect to your bank or to any rideshare or delivery platform, and it does not import fares automatically.

Exports and sharing

PDF reports, CSV exports and backup files are created on your device and only when you ask for them. If you choose to share or save an exported file somewhere - for example to email, a messaging app, or a cloud drive - the handling of that file is governed by the destination you chose, not by My Ride Money.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual records inside the app at any time, or use Settings, then Delete All App Data to erase everything. Deleting the app from your device also removes its data. Unless you exported a backup or other export first, deleted records cannot be recovered - by you or by anyone else.

Children

My Ride Money is a work recordkeeping tool intended for adults who drive for work. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children - it has no collection mechanism at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated in the app and on this page before that version ships, and the privacy summary on the App Store listing will be corrected to match.
