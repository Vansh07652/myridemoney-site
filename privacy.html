Private by design

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

The short version: My Ride Money keeps your records on your device. There is no account, login, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer server holding your data.

What the web app stores

Your profile settings, active shift, completed shifts, earnings, expenses, odometer readings, notes, and preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. The developer cannot see, receive, or recover this information.

Network and offline use

The first visit downloads static app files from GitHub Pages. A service worker caches those files so the tracker can work offline. Your driving records are not included in those requests. GitHub may process standard hosting request information under its own privacy terms.

What the app does not do

It does not request GPS or location access, connect to a bank, automatically import fares, contact a rideshare platform, run analytics, or include third-party advertising or tracking code. Miles are calculated only from odometer readings you enter.

Exports and sharing

CSV reports and JSON backups are created locally only when you request them. Once you save or share an exported file, its handling is governed by the destination you choose.

Deleting data

Delete individual completed shifts from their detail view, clear history, or use Delete all app data in Settings. Clearing browser site data also removes locally stored records. Deleted records cannot be recovered unless you exported a backup.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
